photoflowy Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026 · Last updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clipflowy (the “Developer” or “we”) collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal information of users of the mobile application photoflowy (the “App”). photoflowy is a local-first photo book and ambient photo frame app that manages the photos, photo books, Magic Photobooks, and frame sessions you choose on your device.

1. Summary

  • The photos and photo book files you open (CBZ, CB7, ZIP, PBZ, PB7, images, Markdown notes, .pfslide, and similar files), along with progress, shelves, layouts, captions, notes, search indexes, reminders, and settings, are stored on your device by default and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • We do not read or collect your original photos, photo book contents, search indexes, or AI chat contents for advertising targeting.
  • To provide ads (Google AdMob), remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config), in-app purchases, and optional AI/TTS/Chromecast features, some device information, advertising identifiers, purchase information, or content you explicitly send may be processed by third-party services.
  • External AI, Cloud TTS, external display, Chromecast, photo access, and camera access are used only after your explicit action or operating-system permission.

2.3 Information that may be processed automatically

The App and third-party SDKs may temporarily process general technical information such as IP address, device model, OS version, app version, and language settings to provide the service, ensure security, and diagnose errors. We do not use original photos, photo book contents, or local search indexes for advertising targeting.

4. Advertising and App Tracking Transparency

The App may display ads via Google AdMob. On iOS, it may request your explicit consent for tracking (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, non-personalized ads may be shown instead of personalized ones. We do not directly provide your photos, photo books, search history, AI chats, or reminders for ad personalization. You can manage ad personalization here:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads
  • Google Ad Settings: https://adssettings.google.com

5. Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app data is removed when you delete the App or clear data within the App. Removing an item from the App list usually does not delete the original file you selected; files copied inside the App can be deleted by you. If operating-system device backup is enabled, some local data may be backed up under Apple’s or Google’s backup policies. Retention periods for data handled by third-party services follow those services’ policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13 (or the age defined in your country) and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will delete it without undue delay.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable laws (e.g., the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, EU GDPR, US CCPA), you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal information. Requests can be sent to the contact below.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative safeguards. Sensitive local data such as API keys, authentication tokens, and app-lock information is stored using the operating system’s secure storage (Keychain/Keystore) where possible. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be revised due to legal or service changes; for material changes we will provide notice via the App or this page. The revised Policy takes effect upon posting.
